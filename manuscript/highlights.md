Highlights
- Modern daylight saving time is approximately energy-neutral on average.
- DST sign flips with latitude, heating fuel, AC prevalence, and thermal model.
- Warming reverses DST savings only in a mid-latitude, high-AC band.
- Solar-adaptive scheduling saves ~0.5% relaxed, ~0 under strict constraints.
- Energy- and carbon-optimal schedules nearly coincide even on solar-heavy grids.
